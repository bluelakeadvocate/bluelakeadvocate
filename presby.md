<h2 id="academics">&nbps;<br>Dr. Homer Balabanis To Speak Here Sunday</h2>
<p><i>Blue Lake Advocate, Volume 71, Number 14, 6 August 1959</i></p>
<p>Dr. Homer Balabanis, Dean of Instruction at Humboldt State College, will address the congregation of the First Presbyterian Church this Sunday, August 9, at 10:40 a. m. His subject will be “The Last Gift of Jesus”. An Elder of the Church, Clyde Patenaude, will conduct the Worship Service, and the Summer Choir, conducted by Mrs. Dorothy Jackson, will sing “Beautiful Savior”. All members and non-members are invited to attend.</p>

<h2 id="100years">&nbps;<br>100 Years Ago</h2>
<p><i>Blue Lake Advocate, Volume 39, Number 28, 13 November 1926</i></p>
<p>Preaching service will be held in the Blue Lake Presbyterian church next Sunday, Nov. 14, at 8:00 o’clock. The subject, “Religion is no uncertainty.” Sunday school meets at 10:00 a.m. All are cordially invited to the services of the church. The meeting at Korbel is held at 7:00 p. m. each Sunday evening. Sunday school at 11:00 a. m. All are heartily welcome. The Men’s Brotherhood of the Blue Lake Church will meet next Monday, November 15, at 8:00 p.m.. This will be the first meeting after the summer vacation, and a full attendance is desired to lay plans for the coming winter. The Ladies’ Aid of Blue Lake and Korbel are having some well attended and busy meetings. Final preparations are being made for the annual bazaars. Rev. S. C. GILMAN, Pastor.</p>

<h2 id="dedication">&nbps;<br>Dedication</h2>
<p><i>Blue Lake Advocate, Volume I, Number 7, 12 June 1888</i></p>
<p>On Sunday, June 3d, about one hundred persons assembled at the Protestant church new building in Blue Lake, where dedication services were held. The building lias been handsomely finished inside, papered and painted, as noticed in our last issue, and the aisles have been neatly carpeted. The rostrum is also carpeted, and the pulpit is finished in black walnut. About a hundred light colored chairs have been placed for the seating accommodations, and the interior of the edifice presents a neat, comfortable and appropriate appearance in detail. The church was dedicated for the accommodation and uses of the Presbyterians of this vicinity. The services were conducted by the Rev. J.S. McDonald and Bev. C. H. Emerson. The choir of the Arcata Presbyterian church kindly volunteered for the occasion, and Mr. Greenwood generously leaned his organ for the accompaniments to the vocal music. The dedicatory program consisted of: Voluntary music by the choir, invocation by Rev. C. H. Emerson, hymn by the choir, reading of the scriptures and prayer by Rev. J. S. McDonald, anthem by the choir, sermon by Rev. McDonald, anthem by the choir, dedicatory prayer by Rev. C.H. Emerson, hymn by the choir, benediction. In the afternoon the Blue Lake Presbyterian Church was organized by the Rev. J. S. McDonald. Mr. J. R. Frame was chosen and ordained as ruling elder. After the organization of the congregation, the Rev. (C. H. Emerson, recently from Nebraska, was chosen by the church and congregation to be their minister for one year.</p>
